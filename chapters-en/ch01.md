# Chapter 1: The Tip of the Iceberg

## How Big Is the Real Internet

You use the internet every day. You search on Google. You watch YouTube. You talk on WhatsApp. You feel this is the whole internet.

But the truth is you are only seeing the top part of the internet. Like the tip of an iceberg floating in the sea. From above it looks small, but under the water its real size is many times bigger.

The internet is like that too. What you see is only the beginning.

In this chapter we will understand the basic words in simple language. Because until words like IP address, server and search engine are clear, the talk about the dark web will stay half baked.

### IP Address: Your Home Address on the Internet

When you connect to the internet, your phone or computer gets a number. This is called an IP address.

Understand it like this. Just like every house has an address so the postman can deliver the letter to the right place, in the same way every device on the internet has an address so information can reach the right place. When you open Google, Google's server asks at which address the information should be sent. Your IP address is that address.

From the IP address, a rough idea of your location also comes out. Which city, which internet company. You do not get the exact home address, but this much is definitely known, where you connected from.

This is the reason dark web criminals work so hard to hide their real IP address. Because the moment the IP address is found, the police can reach their city.

### Server: The Godown of the Internet's Shops

You have seen websites. But where does that website live. The answer is on a server.

A server is a big computer that runs day and night. On it are kept all the files, photos and data of the website. When you open a website from your phone, in reality you are asking that server for information.

Take a simple example. You buy goods from a shop. The shopkeeper takes out the goods from the godown and gives them to you. Here the website is the shop and the server is the godown. Your phone is the customer who makes the demand.

The people who run illegal markets on the dark web keep their server hidden. Because if the server is found, the whole market shuts down. The biggest effort of the police is this itself, to find out where the server is.

### How Google Finds Pages

Now one question. How does Google get so many pages. You search anything and lakhs of results come in one second.

Behind this are crawlers. Crawlers are small automatic programs that keep roaming on the internet. They go from one page to another page, read every page and make its record. This is called indexing.

Understand with an example. There is a very big library. It has lakhs of books. The librarian reads every book and makes a list. You see the name in the list and get the book. Google's crawler is that librarian. And the index is that list.

Now understand why the deep web does not show in Google. Because the crawler is not allowed to go there at all. Your email inbox or bank's page is behind a password. The crawler does not have the password, so it cannot put it in the list at all.

And the dark web is completely out of the crawler's reach. The addresses there are such that a normal browser cannot open them at all.

### What Are These Three

To understand the internet, we divide it into three parts.

**First: the clear web.** This is the part you use every day. Google, YouTube, news websites, online shopping. All this is found in Google search. The guess is that this is only a few percent of the whole internet.

**Second: the deep web.** This is the part that does not show in Google, but is not illegal. Your email inbox, the bank's login page, a company's inside records, a hospital's patient data. All this is in the deep web. To see it you need a password, but this is not hidden crime. In reality this is the biggest part of the internet.

**Third: the dark web.** This is the part that does not even open without special software. Here the addresses of websites are also strange. And here hiding your identity is the biggest game.

Understand one thing clearly. The deep web and the dark web are not the same. Your bank account is in the deep web, not in the dark web. Understanding this difference is very important, because in news both are often told as one and the same.

### Dot Onion Addresses

The addresses of dark web websites are not like normal addresses. They end with .onion. And in them there is a long random looking line of letters and numbers.

Understand it like this. A normal website's address is like a shop's signboard. Written clearly, anyone can read it and reach. A .onion address is like a street with no signboard. Only the person knows the address who was told it before.

These addresses are made like this on purpose so that no one can guess whose the website is and where the server is. This is the dark web's identity hiding design.

### The Lie of Films

In films they show hackers. Black hoodie, dark room, green letters running fast on the screen. Bank hacked in ten seconds.

Nothing like this happens in real life.

The real dark web is mostly a market. Stolen data is sold. Fake documents are made. Drugs are bought and sold. This is no magic, it is a way of crime. And like in every crime, here also people make mistakes and get caught.

On the internet another story goes around. "Mariana Web". It is said that below the dark web there is another hidden layer where the governments' deepest secrets are. The truth is there is no proof of this. It is an internet gossip that keeps getting shared again and again. The real dark web itself is big enough and dangerous enough, it does not need stories to make it scarier.

The biggest example is Silk Road. This was the dark web's most famous illegal market. Its owner called himself "Dread Pirate Roberts". He felt no one could catch him. In 2013 America's investigation agency FBI arrested him from a public library. His real name was Ross Ulbricht. (Source: CoinDesk)

The story does not end here. In 2015 an American court gave Ulbricht two life sentences, plus 40 more years. That means he had no way to come out of jail. (Source: Investopedia)

And then on 21 January 2025 American President Donald Trump gave him a full pardon. After spending about 12 years in jail, Ulbricht came out. (Source: Cointelegraph)

This whole story teaches two things. First, even the biggest player of the dark web was caught. Second, both the punishment and the pardon are proof that the dark web is not a world outside the law. The law reaches there too, it just takes time.

That means even the biggest player sitting on the dark web was not saved. Remember this, because in the next chapter we will see how he was caught.

### What Is Sold There

Now the question is what is sold in the dark web's markets. You will be surprised to hear the answer, because out of this many things are your own.

The biggest business is of stolen data. Bundles of emails and passwords. Credit card details. Copies of Aadhaar and PAN cards. Bank account information. All this is bought and sold there.

The second big business is of fake documents. Fake identity cards, fake passports, fake degrees.

And yes, drugs and weapons are also bought and sold. But for a common Indian the biggest danger is the data business itself. Because you will never go to the drugs market, but your data can reach there without your wish.

Know one more thing. In these markets the price of data is shocking. Sometimes bundles of thousands of email-passwords are sold for a few hundred rupees. That means your digital identity is valued very low there. That is why saving it is your own responsibility.

### How Tor Works

The most famous way to reach the dark web is Tor. The easiest way to understand Tor is the onion example.

Suppose you have to send a letter to someone, but you do not want anyone on the way to know who sent the letter and to whom it is going. So you close the letter in three envelopes. The one who opens the first envelope only knows that it has to be delivered to the person of the second envelope. The second one only sees the address of the third. And the one who opens the last envelope gets the real letter, but does not know who the sender was.

Tor does something like this. Your internet traffic passes through three different computers. Every computer only knows the address of the next one. That is why it is called "onion routing". Onion means pyaz. Layer upon layer.

This technique in itself is not illegal. Journalists, social workers and common people in many countries also use it to save their identity. But criminals also take advantage of it.

### So Should We Fear or Not

There is no need to fear the dark web, there is need to understand it.

You do not need to go to the dark web. But you need to know how your data reaches there and how it is sold. Because the purpose of this book is not to make you a hacker. The purpose is to make you so sensible that no one can steal your data and sell it.

For a common man three things are enough. First, do not reveal your IP address and location without reason. Second, keep your passwords strong and different everywhere, so that if one place leaks, the rest of the accounts stay safe. Third, stay away from unknown links and the greed of free things, because data theft often starts from there itself.

In the next chapter we will see how criminals hide their identity. And a more interesting thing. How they get caught.

### Do Not Even Try to Go There by Mistake

Some people think of going to the dark web out of curiosity. This is a very dangerous thinking.

First reason, there is danger on every click there. Fake websites can steal your data. Second reason, just roaming there can also bring you into the police's sight. Third reason, the moment you enter the markets there, you come on the criminals' target.

Curiosity is a good thing, but calm it with books. Stepping on the real dark web is like playing with fire.

**In one line:** The internet you see is only the top part, the real game runs away from your eyes, but every game has rules and the one who breaks the rules gets caught.
