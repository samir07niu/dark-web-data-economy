# Chapter 3: Telegram's Underground Market

## Now the Dark Web Is in Your Pocket

One morning in June 2023, there was panic in India. The news came that there is a bot on Telegram. You put in someone's mobile number, and it gives out that person's name, Aadhaar number, and passport number.

This data was said to be from the CoWIN portal. The same portal where crores of Indians registered for the corona vaccine.

The government said the CoWIN database was not directly hacked. In the early probe by the investigating agency CERT-In, it was said that the bot was pulling information from old stolen data dumps. But the question stayed. How did someone's Aadhaar and passport number reach a Telegram bot. (Source: The Indian Express)

This incident points to a big truth. The dark web is no longer limited to the Tor browser. It has come into apps like Telegram. Into your phone.

### What Is a Bot

A bot is an automatic program that answers messages like a human.

Take an example. You go to a shop and there a machine is fitted. You press a button, put in money, and the goods come out. There is no need to talk to the shopkeeper. Telegram's bot is also such a machine. You send a message, the bot answers by itself.

A bot is not a bad thing by itself. Many companies use bots to answer customers' questions. There are news bots, weather bots. But criminals also use this same technique. Their bots take money and sell stolen data. No human is in the middle, so catching is also difficult.

### Telegram's Encryption Truth

Many people feel that all messages on Telegram are fully safe. This is half truth. Understanding this clearly is very important.

On Telegram there are two types of chats. First is the normal chat. This stays saved on Telegram's server so that you can see your messages even when you change your phone. This chat is encrypted, but the key is also with Telegram. That means if needed, Telegram can read them.

Second is the secret chat. In this there is end-to-end encryption. That means the message only opens in the sender's and the receiver's phone. Even Telegram cannot read in the middle. But you have to start the secret chat yourself. It does not happen by itself. And in group chats this facility is not available at all. (Source: The Hindu)

Now understand. The groups where criminals do business are normal groups. There is no end-to-end encryption there. That means if Telegram wants, it can see those messages and give them to the police. This misunderstanding of criminals often helps them get caught.

### Why Telegram

The question is, why are criminals leaving the dark web and coming to Telegram. The answer is very simple.

First, Telegram is on everyone's phone. Downloading the Tor browser, understanding it, bearing its slow speed. All that is a hassle. Open Telegram and start work. The customers are there, the sellers are there.

Second, making groups and channels on Telegram is easy. One group shuts down, ten new ones open. The police catch one, the rest run away.

Third, bots. Automatic bots can be made on Telegram. The customer messages the bot, pays money, and gets the data. No need to talk to any human. The whole business runs automatically.

### The 99 Rupee Bot

Now one such incident that everyone in India should know.

It was reported in the media that a bot on Telegram was openly selling Indians' private data. For only 99 rupees. You send the bot someone's 10-digit mobile number, and in a few seconds it pulls out that person's full horoscope. Name, father's name, address, Aadhaar number, PAN card details, voter ID. Everything.

First, the tech magazine Digit investigated it. They put in their own team's numbers and checked. The results came out exactly right. Some information was only three-four years old. That means the data was fresh too. (Source: Business Today)

It is believed that this data was collected from many old thefts. From the databases of telecom companies, fintech apps and electricity-water like services. Data stolen over the years was gathered at one place and put behind the bot. Later Telegram removed that bot. (Source: Gadgets360)

Think about it. Someone's full identity was being sold for only 99 rupees. This incident tells how cheap and how easy the data business has become now.

### The Arrest of Telegram's Owner

Now one more big news. On 24 August 2024, Telegram's founder and CEO Pavel Durov was arrested in France. He had landed from his private jet at Paris's Le Bourget Airport, when the French police took him into custody.

12 charges were put on him. The charge was that he did not take the needed steps to stop illegal activities like drug smuggling, fraud and child exploitation on Telegram. After a few days he got bail, but he was stopped from leaving France. (Source: The Hindu)

Telegram said it follows European laws. But this incident gives a big message. Now the world's governments are asking answers from messaging apps. No platform is so big that the law cannot reach it.

For you its meaning is clear. The business happening on Telegram is now in the governments' sight. And the people who buy and sell data there are in more danger than before.

### What Gets Sold in These Groups

I will not tell you the address of these groups. But it is important to know what gets sold there, so that you understand how big the danger is.

The most common business is stolen card data. It is called "carding".

### What Is Carding, in Simple Words

Carding means the wrong use of stolen credit or debit card information.

This work happens in three steps. In the first step the card's details get stolen. From a fake website, a phishing message or the theft of some company's database. In the second step these details are sold on Telegram groups or the dark web. The buyer pays for it in cryptocurrency. In the third step the buyer does online shopping with this information or takes out money. The real card owner comes to know when the message comes from the bank.

The victims of carding are often those people who had put their card number on some unknown link or had told the OTP in some fake call. So the rule is one only. Do not tell your card details and OTP to anyone. The bank never asks for OTP on the phone.

The second business is of data dumps. That means big, big lists. Lists of emails and passwords. Lists of phone numbers. A full database stolen from some company.

The third business is of fraud tools. Kits to make fake websites. Ready-made phishing messages. Such bots that talk like a bank.

And the fourth business is of fake documents.

All this happens in Telegram's private groups and channels. Some have thousands of members.

### The Pizza Search Engine

In 2021, a hacker did something that shook everyone. After Domino's India's data was stolen, he made a website on the dark web. It had a search box on it.

You put in someone's mobile number or email, and it would pull out that person's full pizza order history. Name, address, how much the order was for, when it was ordered. Everything.

Cyber security researcher Rajshekhar Rajaharia saw it first. He told that people were using this data to spy on others. By one evening, about 1.8 crore searches had been done on it. (Source: MediaNama)

Think about it. You once ordered a pizza. And now some stranger can see your address and order history. This is what it means for data to go into wrong hands.

### How This Business Runs

These groups also have a system. Just like a shop.

Sellers have reviews. Old customers tell whether the data was real or fake. Sellers people trust get good ratings.

Money is taken in cryptocurrency. Bitcoin is the most common. Some other coins also run at some places now.

And to avoid cheating, there is an "escrow" system. That means the money first goes to a middleman. When the customer gets the data, then the money goes to the seller.

Hearing all this, it feels like a very professional business. And that is the scariest thing. This is no longer a street-corner crime. It has become an industry.

### What You Can Do

First thing. Stay away from unknown groups and channels on Telegram. If some group tempts you with "free data" or "leaked data", understand that it is a trap. I am not telling you any group's name or how to join here, and you too should stay away from such places.

Second thing. Check your Telegram privacy settings. Who can add you to groups, who can see your number. Keep these settings strong. Keep your profile photo and last seen also limited to contacts only.

Third thing. If you learn that your data has leaked, do not panic. Change your passwords. Tell your bank. And file a complaint on 1930.

Fourth thing. Tell your family too. Especially the elders. Because cheats often target those who know technology less.

### The Fake Customer Care's Telegram Connection

One more trick that runs a lot on Telegram. You search a company's customer care number on Google. You get a number that is not real. You call or message on it.

On the other side a cheat is sitting. He asks for your details. Sometimes he tells you to come on Telegram "for a quick solution". There he sends you a fake form or app link. The moment you fill it, your data is with him.

The way to save yourself is easy. Always take the customer care number from the company's real website. Do not trust the number shown at the top of Google, because fake numbers also come in ads there. And no real company will ask you to come on Telegram and fill a form.

**In one line:** The dark web is no longer in some hidden lane, it is inside an app on your phone, so keep control over your finger.
